# Cognize / StreakPeak — Weekly Review UI Specification

> Analyzed from screenshots provided in the `/home` folder on 2026-08-14.
> This feature is accessed via the "Weekly Review" button at the bottom of the Home screen.

---

## Overview
The Weekly Review is a data visualization dashboard that summarizes the user's performance across tasks, habits, focus time, and mood for the past week, ending with reflection prompts.

---

## 1. Header & Summary
- **Header**: `<` Back button. Small caps `WEEKLY REVIEW`. Large bold date range (e.g., `Aug 10 - Aug 16`).
- **Summary Card**: 
  - Title: Dynamic text based on performance (e.g., `Outstanding week. 🔥`).
  - Subtitle: E.g., `You completed 100% of your tasks this week.`

---

## 2. Key Metrics Grid
A 3-column row of small metric cards:
1. **Streak**: ⚡ Lightning bolt icon (gold). Value (e.g., `3d`). Label `Streak`.
2. **Focus**: ⏳ Hourglass icon (blue). Value (e.g., `0m`). Label `Focus`.
3. **Sessions**: 🔄 Sync/loop icon (green). Value (e.g., `0`). Label `Sessions`.

---

## 3. Completion Charts
A 2-column row of donut charts:
1. **Tasks**: 
   - Label: `Tasks`
   - Visualization: A thick blue circular progress ring (donut chart) with the percentage in the center (e.g., `100%`).
   - Subtitle: e.g., `1 of 1 done`.
2. **Habits**:
   - Label: `Habits`
   - Visualization: A thick green circular progress ring with percentage in center (e.g., `0%`).
   - Subtitle: e.g., `0 streaks active`.

---

## 4. Daily Activity Bar Chart
- **Title**: `DAILY ACTIVITY`.
- **Legend**: 🔵 Tasks (Blue), 🟢 Focus (Green).
- **Visualization**: A bar chart with the days of the week (`M`, `T`, `W`, `T`, `F`, `S`, `S`) on the X-axis.
- **Data Series**:
  - Tasks: Rendered as stacked vertical blue bars.
  - Focus: Rendered as a green horizontal line overlay or secondary bar.

---

## 5. Mood Trends
- **Header**: 😊 `Mood Trends`. Right-aligned text: `Avg: 4.0/5` (Blue).
- **Visualization**: A line graph plotting mood scores (1-5) across the days of the week.
- **Insight Text**: Muted, italicized text at the bottom. e.g., *"Your mood affects your productivity. When you feel good, you are more likely to complete your habits!"*

---

## 6. Insights & Reflection
- **Most Productive Card**:
  - Icon: 🏆 Trophy icon on a gold/brown squircle background.
  - Label: `MOST PRODUCTIVE` (small caps).
  - Value: The day name (e.g., `Friday` in large bold text).
  - Subtitle: Stats for that day (e.g., `1 tasks · 0m focus`).
- **Reflect Card**:
  - Distinctive styling: A dark blue-tinted card background.
  - Header: 💡 `REFLECT` (small caps, blue).
  - Content: A bulleted list of 3 dynamic reflection questions (e.g., *"What helped you stay on top of your tasks this week?", "Which habit do you want to protect first next week?"*).
  - *Note: These appear to be read-only prompts for journaling elsewhere, as there are no text inputs provided in this card.*

## Open Questions

> 2. **Reflection Prompts**: Are the reflection questions static, or are they generated by AI based on the user's weekly performance? If AI-generated, we need to wire up an OpenAI call here.
